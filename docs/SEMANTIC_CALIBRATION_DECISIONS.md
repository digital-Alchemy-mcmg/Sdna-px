# Spatial DNA semantic calibration — decision record

Status: governing implementation decisions for semantic calibration.

## Trust boundary

Spatial DNA is not a background-check or resume-audit service. Candidate-supplied canonical facts are authoritative input after candidate compilation. Lack of outside corroboration is not contradictory evidence. Garbage in can produce garbage out; fabrication introduced by Spatial DNA is a system defect.

Contradictions are different from missing corroboration. Candidate compilation must surface and resolve contradictory source facts before the compiled DNA is used for projection. Projection must not silently average, prefer, or normalize contradictory facts.

## Static DNA, dynamic projection

Compiled Spatial DNA is read-only during a job projection. The target changes the lens, traversal, gravity, active receptors, and expression; it does not mutate candidate DNA.

New candidate evidence is appended through controlled recompilation. It is cross-referenced against the existing substrate for contradictions and may create new corroborating relationships. Existing evidence is not rewritten merely to fit a target.

Chronology remains an attribute, not the default ranking axis.

## Projection-relative gravity

No atom has a permanent gravity or job-fit rank. Gravity is evaluated fresh for every target from Set Zero through the target lens. Different jobs can activate different combinations of the six canonical planes.

## Evidence authority and semantic magnitude are separate

Do not collapse these into one score.

- Objective, authoritative facts can carry full source authority.
- A documented event can be fully authoritative as an event while an interpretive proposition derived from it remains subjective.
- Candidate-source facts already admitted to the canonical record are usable as authoritative source data unless actual contradictory evidence exists.
- CANDIDATE-REPORTED is provenance, not an automatic numerical penalty.

## Soft / interpretive evidence

Self-reference alone contributes 0% to an interpretive or latent claim.

For independent, semantically convergent, non-self-referential sources:
- one independent source: 25%
- two independent sources: 40%
- three or more independent sources: 50%
- 50% is the hard ceiling for the soft or interpretive category.

The progression belongs to the semantic attribute cluster, not to each document. Corroboration propagates only where independent evidence converges on the same latent proposition. Semantic proximity may identify candidate relationships; it does not by itself prove corroboration.

## Semantically loaded quantitative language

Terms such as high volume are contextual categories, not universal numerical truths. If the target supplies no explicit quantitative anchor, the phrase is interpreted in the relevant operating context. If the target supplies a number, that number can anchor the target-side comparison.

Candidate-side baseline and ceiling establish whether evidence belongs to the category and its magnitude. Example: if $9M is established candidate high-volume experience and the calibrated baseline is $4.5M, values below the baseline do not support the high-volume category.

An explicit hard gate is different. High-volume experience is semantic. Must have managed $10.7M+ annual sales is quantitative and must be satisfied as written.

Evidence materially above the target requirement may authorize stronger natural framing. No adjective lookup table is required.

## Renderer / outward claim gate

The renderer may choose the strongest useful formulation inside the evidence envelope. It may emphasize, abstract, and frame favorably. It may not invent or materially misrepresent an event, credential, number, responsibility, outcome, or capability.

Final gate:

**Is the statement defensible without retreat? Is the statement not untrue?**

The ordinary interpretation by a reasonable reader must survive an obvious follow-up. If the candidate must rescue the statement with a hidden qualifier or materially narrower reinterpretation, the wording fails the gate.

This is a resume system, not an SAT or a formal model of every adjective in English. Calibration should be sufficient to produce useful, defensible projections; observed failures are inputs to later recalibration.
